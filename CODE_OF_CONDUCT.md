# Code of Conduct

PageSmithMD follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

## Our pledge
We want taking part in this project to be a friendly, harassment-free experience for everyone,
whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race,
religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect
- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Respect different opinions and experiences, and give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to anyone affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what's best for the project and its users.

## What isn't acceptable
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope
This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and when
someone is representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting
If someone's behaviour worries you, report it privately to the maintainers through GitHub: use the
**⋯ > Report content** option on the comment, issue or pull request. All reports are reviewed
promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the person reporting is respected.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that
don't follow this Code of Conduct, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose
behaviour is inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful, following the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
